What the dataset shows

Rank My Prop currently contains 22 published firm records, 12 active offer records, 91 published account programs and 74 detailed program rulebooks. 15 firms currently have at least twelve structured data points across key metrics, trading conditions and firm details. Coverage is not treated as proof that a firm is safe, profitable or suitable for every trader.

The approved community-review dataset contains 21 records. 9 currently include a public proof attachment. A missing attachment does not automatically make a review false, and an attachment does not independently prove every claim in a submission.

Methodology

  1. Only active, published CMS firm and offer records are counted.
  2. Community reviews must have an approved status before inclusion.
  3. Program and rulebook counts come from structured records, not promotional headline claims.
  4. Missing values remain missing; Rank My Prop does not infer them from unrelated firms.
  5. The report date changes only when a source record receives a material update.

Limitations and citation

Prop firm rules, offers and platform availability can change. This snapshot documents Rank My Prop's published records at the stated update date and is not financial advice or a guarantee of payouts. Verify material terms with the relevant firm.

Suggested citation: Rank My Prop, “Prop Firm Market Snapshot 2026,” updated 28 September 2026. When citing the data online, link to this report page.
