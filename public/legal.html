01

Entity and Platform Scope

Rank My Prop is an informational platform focused on prop firm comparisons, offer tracking, educational resources, and trader tools. Unless explicitly stated in writing, Rank My Prop does not act as a broker, prop firm, portfolio manager, or investment intermediary. References to third-party firms, brands, logos, or products are for identification and informational comparison purposes.

02

Intellectual Property and Brand Protection

Trademarks, design elements, content structure, code implementation, illustrations, copy, and database presentation used on Rank My Prop are protected by applicable intellectual property law. Unauthorized use, republication, automated extraction, or replication of proprietary sections, rankings, and editorial frameworks may lead to access restriction, takedown requests, or legal enforcement.

03

User-Generated Content and Moderation

Users may submit reviews, claims, referral details, support notes, and related profile content through dashboard modules. By submitting content, you grant Rank My Prop a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license to host, display, moderate, and process it for operational purposes. We may edit, reject, withhold, or remove content that appears inaccurate, abusive, unlawful, promotional, or policy-violating.

04

Compliance and Investigative Cooperation

Rank My Prop may preserve and disclose account or submission records where required for legal compliance, fraud investigation, dispute handling, regulatory requests, law-enforcement cooperation, chargeback defense, or internal risk control. Workflows may be paused when verification records are incomplete, inconsistent, or under review for abuse.

05

Jurisdiction and Dispute Handling

Website materials and platform operations are governed by applicable laws of the operating jurisdiction of Rank My Prop, subject to mandatory consumer-protection provisions where legally required. A dispute arising from use of the website should first be raised through good-faith written communication. If unresolved, it may be handled by a competent court as permitted by law.

06

Policy Hierarchy

This legal overview is read together with the Disclaimer, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookies Policy. Where overlap exists, the more specific policy for the relevant issue controls. We may update a policy to reflect platform evolution, legal obligations, or risk controls.

07

Contact and Official Notices

For legal notices, intellectual-property concerns, moderation escalations, or compliance communication, contact Rank My Prop through the official channel above. Include complete context, relevant URLs, and evidence where applicable so the request can be reviewed efficiently.
