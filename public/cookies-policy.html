Privacy policy
01

Cookies and Browser Storage We Use

Rank My Prop may use cookies, local storage, session storage and similar browser technologies. These technologies can store a small identifier or preference on your device so the website can maintain a secure session, remember interface choices, support account workflows and avoid repeating settings unnecessarily.

02

Essential Technologies

Essential storage supports functions such as authentication, account security, fraud prevention, navigation state, dashboard continuity and user-requested preferences. These technologies are required for parts of the platform to operate. Disabling them may cause sign-in, saved journal data, forms or other account features to stop working as expected.

03

Analytics and Performance

We may use privacy-conscious analytics or performance technologies to understand aggregate visits, page performance, feature usage and technical errors. This information helps us improve navigation, page speed and reliability. Analytics configuration can change as the platform evolves, and available browser or consent controls will apply where required.

04

Third-Party Services

Authentication, hosting, database, media, email or analytics providers may set or read technologies needed to deliver their service. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and the configuration used by Rank My Prop. External prop firm websites reached through our links operate independently and may use different cookies and tracking technologies.

05

Your Choices and Controls

Most browsers allow you to view, block or delete cookies and website data. You can also use private-browsing controls or device settings where available. Deleting website data may sign you out and remove locally stored preferences. Browser controls do not delete server-side account records; privacy or deletion requests should be submitted through our contact page.

06

Policy Updates and Contact

We may update this policy when storage practices, service providers or legal requirements change. The revised date will appear at the top of this page. For questions about cookies, browser storage or privacy, contact rankmyprop@gmail.com.
