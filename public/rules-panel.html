Rules
Read and understand all the rules before starting your challenge.
Rule Categories

General Rules

Important rules you must follow to successfully pass the challenge.
1
Provide accurate information during registration.

All information provided during registration must be true and accurate. We reserve the right to verify your identity and details.

2
One account per trader.

Multiple accounts are not allowed. Any additional accounts found will result in disqualification of all accounts.

3
Complete the evaluation within the given time limit.

You must complete all challenge phases within the specified time limit. Extensions are not provided.

4
Follow all trading rules and risk management guidelines.

Your account must comply with all trading rules, risk management parameters, and restrictions outlined in our rules.

5
Violating rules may result in account termination.

Any violation of our rules may result in immediate account termination and forfeiture of funds.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Trading Rules

Trading guidelines and restrictions for all challenge accounts.
1
No holding positions over the weekend.

All positions must be closed before market close on Friday. Holding trades over the weekend is strictly prohibited.

2
Minimum trading days requirement.

You must trade on a minimum number of days as specified in your challenge plan to qualify for evaluation.

3
Use only approved trading instruments.

Only instruments listed in our approved trading list may be used. Trading unlisted instruments is not permitted.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Risk Management

Risk parameters and limitations you must adhere to at all times.
1
Maximum daily loss limit.

You must not exceed the maximum daily drawdown limit as specified in your challenge plan. Breaching this results in immediate failure.

2
Overall maximum drawdown.

The total account drawdown must never exceed the overall maximum loss limit at any point during the challenge.

3
Position sizing limits apply.

Each trade must adhere to the maximum lot size and risk per trade requirements outlined in your challenge details.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Prohibited Activities

Activities that are strictly not allowed on our platform.
1
No high-frequency or latency arbitrage trading.

Strategies that exploit latency or price feed differences are not permitted under any circumstances.

2
No use of third-party signal services.

Purchasing or subscribing to external signal providers to pass the challenge is prohibited.

3
No account sharing or group trading.

The account must be traded solely by the registered account holder. Sharing access is a violation.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

News Trading

Rules governing trading around major economic news events.
1
No trading 2 minutes before/after high-impact news.

Opening or closing positions within 2 minutes of a high-impact news event is prohibited to prevent exploitation of volatility.

2
Follow the economic calendar restrictions.

Refer to our approved economic calendar for restricted periods. Trading during blackout windows will result in trade invalidation.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Expert Advisors

Rules regarding the use of automated trading systems.
1
EAs must comply with all platform rules.

Any automated trading system used must adhere to all trading and risk management rules without exception.

2
No tick scalping EAs allowed.

Expert Advisors that use tick scalping or ultra-short strategies exploiting spread are prohibited.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Copy Trading

Rules around copying or mirroring trades from other accounts.
1
Copy trading between own accounts is prohibited.

Using copy trading software to mirror trades from one personal account to another is not allowed.

2
Third-party copy services are not permitted.

Subscribing to any external copy trading platform to trade your challenge account is strictly forbidden.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Payout Rules

Requirements and conditions for receiving your profit payouts.
1
Minimum payout threshold must be reached.

Payouts are only processed once the minimum profit threshold defined in your plan has been achieved.

2
Payout requests are processed within 5 business days.

Once a valid payout request is submitted, processing takes up to 5 business days.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.

Other Rules

Additional rules and policies that apply to all accounts.
1
Chargebacks will result in permanent bans.

Initiating a chargeback for any payment made to us will result in immediate and permanent account termination.

2
Rules are subject to change without prior notice.

We reserve the right to update or modify these rules at any time. It is your responsibility to stay informed.

These rules are subject to change. Please review regularly.
Rule Violations

Violating any of our rules may result in:

  • Account termination
  • Forfeiture of all funds
  • Disqualification from future challenges
  • Legal action if necessary
Important Note

It is your responsibility to read, understand, and follow all the rules. We recommend reviewing these rules regularly as they may be updated from time to time.
