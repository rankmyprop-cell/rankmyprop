## Legitimate plan (no paid/PBN/automated links)
| Tactic | What | Why | How | Pri | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original data reports | Quarterly "Prop Firm Rule Changes", "Fee vs refund index", "Payout methods available to Indian traders" built from your DB | Linkable, citable by AI answers | Publish with methodology + CSV; pitch to trading/fintech writers | P2 | Referring domains per report |
| Indian fintech/finance media | Contributed data commentary (not paid placements) | India relevance | Pitch to editors/columnists; disclose any commercial relationship | P3 | Editorial links |
| Trading communities | Helpful answers in r/Forex, r/FuturesTrading, Discord/Telegram groups; your own Discord | Brand mentions, referral traffic | Disclose affiliation; no link-dropping; follow each community's rules | P3 | Branded searches, referral sessions |
| Industry directories | Listings where legitimate (business directories, Product Hunt-style, fintech lists) | Entity consistency | Match name/URL/description exactly | P3 | Directory links |
| Prop-firm partnerships | Firms linking to their listing/review on your site, joint rule explainers | Relevant links | Offer verified data pages, not paid reviews | P2 | Links from firm sites |
| Tool-led links | Embeddable calculators (drawdown, cost) with attribution | Natural citations | Ship the tool, make a landing page, add "cite/embed" snippet | P2 | Tool backlinks |
| YouTube/shorts | Short rule explainers pointing to pages | Mentions + traffic | Match topics to guides | P3 | Views → site clicks |
| HARO-style/expert quotes | Respond to journalist queries on prop trading | Authority links | Named expert with credentials | P3 | Placements |
| Broken-link outreach | Replace dead resources on trading blogs | Low-cost links | Only when your page genuinely fits | P3 | Links won |

## Do not
Buy links, join link exchanges, use link farms/PBNs, mass-comment, or publish unlabeled sponsored posts. Affiliate/paid relationships must be disclosed and tagged `rel="sponsored"`.

## Brand/entity consistency
Same name ("PropFirmMine"), legal entity text (footer says "PropFirmMine Technologies Ltd." [V] — keep identical in About, schema and social profiles), logo, Discord/X/YouTube handles.
