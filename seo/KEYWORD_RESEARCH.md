# Keyword Research — PropFirmMine

## Data policy
No keyword tool (GSC, Ahrefs, Semrush, Keyword Planner) is connected, so **volume and difficulty are NOT available and are left blank, not guessed.** Two data points are sourced:
- ~1,800 monthly US searches "best futures prop firms" vs ~400 "best forex prop firms"; futures demand +108% vs 2024 — *Prop Firm Compare press release, Oct 27 2026, company-reported, US only.*
- India SERP neighbours (fxempire, india.com, thehansindia, thetrustedprop, PFM) confirm commercial intent for "best prop firms in India" style queries (existence only, not volume).

**To fill the blanks:** export 90-day queries from GSC (after fixing T1–T4), then pull volumes/KD from Ahrefs/Semrush for the lists below.

Columns: Intent (C = commercial investigation, T = transactional, I = informational) · Vol/KD = **needs tool** · Opportunity is a judgement [E] · Priority P1–P3.
